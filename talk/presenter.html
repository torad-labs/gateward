Presenter view

This mirrors your speaker notes and stays in sync with deck.html. Put the deck on the projector (press F there for fullscreen) and keep this on your laptop.

To link them: open deck.html and this presenter.html from the same browser (both as file://, or both served from the same local server). Navigate with the arrow keys in either window — they move together.

If the dot up top stays grey, your browser is blocking cross-window messaging for local files; serve the folder with bunx serve talk (or any static server) and open both from http://localhost.
